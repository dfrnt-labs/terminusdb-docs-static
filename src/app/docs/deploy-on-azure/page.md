TerminusDB is available on Azure through the Azure Marketplace: a VM image that deploys into your own subscription and bills through Azure.

{% cta-link href="https://azuremarketplace.microsoft.com/en/marketplace/apps/terminusdb1606769310168.terminusdb_vm" event="azure_marketplace" %}Open the TerminusDB listing on Azure Marketplace{% /cta-link %}

## Early access to managed deployments

For early access to managed TerminusDB deployments — on **Azure**, **AWS**, or **Google Cloud** — and for other ways of running TerminusDB Enterprise in production, {% cta-link href="https://gettier.ai/connect" event="azure_contact" %}contact the team at Gettier{% /cta-link %}.

Early access covers:

- Managed TerminusDB Enterprise in your preferred cloud and region
- [Enterprise document formats](/docs/enterprise-document-formats/) (Turtle, RDF/XML, JSON-LD), [observability](/docs/enterprise-observability/), and [backup tooling](/docs/enterprise-backup-restore/)
- Deployment assistance, sizing, and support options

## Self-managed alternative

TerminusDB is open source under Apache 2.0 and runs anywhere Docker does. To run it yourself today, see [self-hosted installation](/docs/self-hosted-installation/) or [Kubernetes](/docs/install-on-kubernetes/). All hosting options are collected on the [TerminusDB Cloud](/docs/cloud/) page.
